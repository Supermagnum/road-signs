# Trafikkalfabetet (Traffic Alphabet) — English translation

This is an English translation of the **textual** rules in Statens vegvesen’s annex PDF
[`trafikkalfabetet.pdf`](trafikkalfabetet.pdf) (N300 attachment). The PDF remains the
authoritative document for letterform drawings and dimension tables (figures V2.1–V2.3).

**Official Norwegian source:**  
https://www.vegvesen.no/globalassets/fag/handboker/vedlegg-til-n300/trafikkalfabetet.pdf

**Background (not from the PDF):** Trafikkalfabetet is the typeface developed for text on
Norwegian public traffic signs. It was designed at Vegdirektoratet around 1965
(Karl Petter Sandbæk; project lead Karsten Krogsæter). It was also used on Norwegian
vehicle registration plates until 2002. Digitised versions for sign producers exist
separately from this official pattern PDF.

---

## What Trafikkalfabetet is

Trafikkalfabetet is a dedicated typeface developed for text on traffic signs. Patterns for
the design of letters and digits are given in figures **V2.1 a–c** in the PDF.

The text characters on the figures extend from the inner edge of the strokes that bound
the characters. Characters are dimensioned as fractions of the height of capital letters,
**H**. Widths of letters and digits for the various standardised height groups are given
in figure **V2.2**.

The Trafikkalfabetet character patterns can be ordered from Vegdirektoratet.

---

## Use of text on signs

Trafikkalfabetet **shall be used for all text** on public traffic signs, except:

- variable pixel / matrix signs (see chapters 1–4.3 in the handbook series), and
- sign **590** “Skilt med trafikksikkerhetsinformasjon” (signs with traffic-safety
  information) — see Del 3.

Letter text shall be set with an initial capital and lowercase letters otherwise, unless
something else is specified for particular signs.

Spacing between characters in the same word or group and on the same line is given in
figures **V2.3 a–j**.

### Spacing rules (in terms of capital height H)

| Rule | Spacing |
|------|---------|
| Between words | **5/7 H** |
| Between a number and its unit/label on the same line | **4/7 H** |
| Between numbers separated by a hyphen | **H** |
| When a comma, parenthesis, etc. is inserted between numbers | Increase the usual digit spacing as judged appropriate |

### Line spacing

Unless otherwise specified for the individual sign, the distance between text baselines
(the imaginary lines on which letters and digits sit) shall be:

| Content | Line spacing |
|---------|--------------|
| Text lines with symbols | **14/7 H** |
| Text only (letters and digits) | **12/7 H** |
| Split text (word or name) | **11/7 H** |

“Split text” may be long words or names divided with a hyphen, and compound names such as
“Løkkedalen sykehjem”.

If only one of two text lines contains symbols, use line spacing **14/7 H**.
